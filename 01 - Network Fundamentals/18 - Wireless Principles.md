# Wireless Principles

## Main terms
- **SSID:** human-readable wireless network name; it does not by itself provide security.
- **RF:** radio-frequency signals; affected by distance, attenuation, interference and obstacles.
- **BSSID:** identifier of a specific access point radio/cell, commonly represented by a MAC address.
- **Channels:** 2.4 GHz commonly uses nonoverlapping channels 1, 6 and 11 where permitted; 5/6 GHz offer more channels and different regulatory constraints.
- **Encryption:** use modern authentication and encryption (WPA2-AES or WPA3) rather than WEP or TKIP.

## What to check
Low speeds may come from overlapping channels, congested airtime, weak signal, excessive clients, or the link between AP and switch. A strong signal does not always mean good throughput.
