# AI and ML in Network Operations

- **Machine learning (ML):** learns patterns from data (e.g., baselining normal interface utilisation).
- **Predictive AI:** estimates likely future events (e.g., link congestion or hardware anomalies).
- **Generative AI:** generates or explains text/configuration proposals from prompts and context.

## Useful tasks
- Alert correlation and incident summaries.
- Capacity forecasting and anomaly detection.
- Drafting configuration or troubleshooting suggestions.

## Risks
Incomplete telemetry, hallucinated commands, sensitive logs and false positives can produce bad recommendations. Validate against device support, change controls and observed results; do not apply AI-generated commands blindly.
