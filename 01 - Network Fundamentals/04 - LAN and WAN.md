# LAN and WAN

- **LAN (Local Area Network):** a local network, such as home, office or school. Usually managed by one organisation.
- **WAN (Wide Area Network):** links sites over a broader region using ISP, leased line, fibre, MPLS, VPN or another network service.

```text
Office A LAN -- Router -- WAN / ISP -- Router -- Office B LAN
```

A switch connects local devices; a router forwards IP packets between different networks.

## Typical WAN media
Ethernet handoff, broadband, optical fibre, serial in older training and provider-managed circuits.
